The move itself is part of the price

A move to Mexico is never just Mexico's tax rules. Leaving Norway with unrealised gains can trigger the exit tax, as if you sold on the way out, and Runway prices that into the plan before a single MXN of spending is modelled. The mechanics are in what the exit tax costs when you leave Norway, and the wider tooling question in which FIRE calculators handle a move abroad.

Once you are there, your spending runs in MXN through an exchange-rate scenario you control, so a weak krone decade is a plan you can stress, not a surprise.

Frequently asked

What tax does Runway assume on investment gains in Mexico?+

Runway applies Mexico's 10 percent flat rate to gains on listed shares and taxes rental income on the progressive scale. The figures are 2026, income-year-stamped, and refreshed as rules change.

Do I still pay Norway's exit tax if I retire to Mexico?+

Yes. Leaving Norway with unrealised gains can trigger utflyttingsskatt, and Runway prices it into every plan that retires to Mexico, as a cost of the move, before the destination's own rules take over on the drawdown.

What rate does Mexico charge on share gains?+

A flat 10 percent on gains from listed shares, which is what Runway applies to a typical fund-and-share drawdown. Unlisted assets follow different rules, which is a pack-manifest detail rather than something the headline hides.
